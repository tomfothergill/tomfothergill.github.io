About

I’m a 29-year-old lead AI engineer at Hyperfinity, working in Leeds. In my day job I work primarily with retail companies, building applications that help people make sense of their business data. I’m a one-person AI team, so my work spans the full stack: developing agents, building the backend, managing cloud infrastructure and designing and implementing the frontend.

About this site

Some of these articles cover things I’ve built in my professional life; others are about personal projects. I write with AI’s help, working through the ideas and editing the drafts until I’m happy putting my name to them. The articles are a way of looking back at the work and explaining the decisions I made along the way.
